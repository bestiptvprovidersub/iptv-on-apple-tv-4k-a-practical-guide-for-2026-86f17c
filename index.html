This guide shows you how to set up IPTV on your Apple TV 4K in 2026. You need a premium IPTV subscription and a compatible player app.

Follow the steps below to start streaming.

What You Need

  • Apple TV 4K (any generation) with the latest tvOS
  • A premium IPTV subscription like SubTV IP (available at subtvip.store) – instant activation after purchase
  • A supported player app: TiviMate 5.x, IBO Player Pro, IPTV Smarters Pro, Kodi 21, or Flex IPTV (download from App Store)
  • Stable internet connection (Wi-Fi or Ethernet)
  • Optional: Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1) to reduce ISP throttling

Step-by-Step Setup

  1. Get your SubTV IP subscription. Go to subtvip.store, choose a 1‑, 3‑, or 12‑month plan. Pay securely with PayPal or a credit card. Your account activates instantly.
  2. Install a player app on Apple TV 4K. Open the App Store on your Apple TV, search for one of the supported players (e.g. IPTV Smarters Pro), and install it.
  3. Set up Cloudflare DNS for anti‑throttling. On your Apple TV, go to “Settings > Network > Wi‑Fi”. Select your network, choose “Configure DNS” and switch it to “Manual”. Enter 1.1.1.1 as the primary DNS and 1.0.0.1 as the secondary. Press the Back button to save.
  4. Open your player app and log in. Use the username and password (or M3U URL) provided in your SubTV IP welcome message. If the app asks for a portal URL, enter the one from your account.
  5. Adjust the buffer/cache setting. Most players have a buffer option in their settings. Set it to 5,000 ms – this matches the recommended value from SubTV IP to prevent freezing.
  6. Start watching. After login, a channel list or VOD menu will appear. Select any stream and play it. Use the Apple TV remote to navigate.

If a Step Fails

  • Check your internet speed – a minimum of 25 Mbps is recommended for 4K streams.
  • Re‑enter your login credentials. Double‑check username/password from your welcome email.
  • Restart the Apple TV and the player app.
  • Contact SubTV IP via WhatsApp (provided in your account) for instant help.
  • Visit general streaming guides on sites like Polygon or Gizmodo for additional troubleshooting tips.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is IPTV and how does it work on Apple TV 4K?

IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. It delivers TV channels and video on demand over the internet instead of cable or satellite. On Apple TV 4K you use a player app to enter your subscription details and watch streams.

Which player app is best for Apple TV 4K in 2026?

All supported players work well. IPTV Smarters Pro and IBO Player Pro are very popular on Apple TV. TiviMate 5.x offers advanced features but is more common on Android. Choose the one you find easiest to use.

Do I need to change the DNS on my Apple TV?

Changing to Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1) helps reduce buffering caused by your internet provider. It’s optional but recommended for smooth 4K streaming.

How do I set the buffer to 5,000 ms?

Open your player app, go to “Settings” or “Preferences”, look for “Buffer Size”, “Cache”, or “Stream Buffer”. Enter 5000 (milliseconds). Some apps label it as 5 seconds – that’s the same.
